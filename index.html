English for young learners · Leveled reading

Listen, read, and rebuild
English books at your step

Children listen to and read a book at their own step, then rebuild its sentences from meaning chunks. Teachers see where a child gets stuck, not just a score. There is nothing to install and no sign-up.

How children learn here

Read one now

A library in steps

Listening and word meanings

When a sentence is read aloud, the word being read is marked. Tap a word to hear it and see a simple English meaning. With "Word help" at the bottom of the page, the meaning also shows in Chinese or Korean.

Rebuild the sentence

Sentences from the book are cut into meaning chunks and mixed up, and the child puts them back in order. While reading, "Chunk reading" shows the chunks first.

Lesson plans and print books

Each book comes with activities for before, while and after reading, and can be printed as a paper book.

Reading stamp card

There are no points to collect. Each book has four stamps (Listen, Read, Rebuild, Check), and four finished books in a step take you to the next step. The other books stay open.

See where reading gets hard

Tapped words go into "My word book", and pages with a wrong answer or a rebuild mistake are noted. In the Check, a wrong answer sends the child back to the page, not to the answer.

Quiet screens, natural pictures

Backgrounds and cards use one color, and color is used in three places only: blue for things to tap, a purple underline for the letters to learn, and yellow for rewards.

No tracking, no server

There are no ads or analytics scripts. Records and recordings stay on this device, and a teacher only gets the record code that the student makes.

About this prototype: it has books for Steps 1 to 6. New students start with "Find my step" in the library. The teacher page shows real records when student record codes are imported, and sample data when there are none. The voice is the browser's built-in English voice.
